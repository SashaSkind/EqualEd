# EqualEd demo script (90 seconds)

**Setup before going on stage (2 min):** laptop on the table facing the presenter's chair, `open -a EqualEd`,
dashboard open on the laptop, professor page open on a phone (same Wi-Fi or phone hotspot), tap
"Tap once to turn on alert sound and pop-ups" on the phone. Dashboard > Settings: turn ON "Play sounds on the
laptop speakers" so the judges hear the calming sound. Two teammates standing off to the side.

| Time | Say | Do | Judges see |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "1 in 36 US kids is autistic, and for most of them sensory overload, not the lesson, decides whether they can stay in a regular classroom." | Sit at the laptop. | Camera window, calm. |
| 0:10 | "EqualEd runs on the student's own laptop. I raise my hands so it locks onto me, and only me." | Raise both hands 1 s. | Green "LOCKED ON" banner, oval bubble around you. |
| 0:20 | "It watches the bubble around me, not the whole room." | Teammate A walks around far away. | Busy meter stays low. Nothing fires. |
| 0:30 | "Now the room closes in." | Teammates A and B walk up and stay beside you. | Labels "TOO CLOSE", "STAYING CLOSE 5s", red lines, busy meter climbs. |
| 0:40 | "The teacher gets it on their phone, before I react." | Hold up the phone. | Pop-up: "Someone has stayed close to the student", "Very busy around them". Calming sound starts. |
| 0:55 | "And if I do get overwhelmed..." | Cover both ears, or rock. | "Covering ears" turns red, overload alert, professor taps "I'm on my way", laptop shows "ON THE WAY". |
| 1:05 | "Everything you just saw ran on this laptop. Nothing left the room." | | |
| 1:10 | "We also ran it over the VAST archive: NVIDIA Cosmos described 2,352 clips, and we scored every one for sensory load." | Dashboard > Sensory map (VAST). | Places ranked: SF streets most overwhelming, neighborhood calmest. |
| 1:20 | "So a school can see which places and times will overwhelm a student before they ever go there." | (If Cosmos key is in) point at the "NVIDIA Cosmos sees" card. | Cosmos's live read of the room. |
| 1:30 | "EqualEd: spotting sensory overload before it happens." | | |

**If something fails live:** press `s` (simulates an overload, alert goes to the phone), or play the backup
recording. Record it beforehand: press `r` in the camera window, run the demo once, press `r` again
(saved in `recordings/`).

**Questions to expect:**
- *Privacy?* Video never leaves the laptop. Only the enrolled student is tracked; everyone else is an anonymous box.
- *What if every student is anxious?* Each student has their own lock, bubble, baseline and trigger profile ("Bothered me / Was fine" buttons).
- *Is it a medical device?* No. It is a support tool for teachers and IEP aides.
