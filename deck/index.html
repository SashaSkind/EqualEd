The camera sees the blender first.

Calming sound fades in before the noise.

The teacher’s voice stays on top in Maya’s AirPods.

Then the microphone confirms it.

A loudness reflex fires in milliseconds. A sound classifier names the sound a moment later. Each warning is logged with how early it came.

Same engine. Three rules.

Autism

Sensory shield

Spots what is about to get loud or crowded, warns early, and alerts the teacher if Maya shows signs of overload.

ADHD

Attention rewind

Marks the moments a student looked away and answers “what did I miss about mitochondria?” from the transcript.

Dyslexia

Read-along

Lean in toward a hard paragraph and it is read aloud.

How it is built

See

Camera + mic

Laptop webcam or a thumb-sized XIAO ESP32S3 camera.

Understand

YOLO11 + NVIDIA Cosmos3-Reason

Objects, then “what is about to happen?” on CoreWeave GPUs.

Remember

VAST DataEngine + VastDB

Every moment indexed, so “show every time she was overwhelmed” is one search.

Answer

W&B Inference + Weave

“What did I miss?” answers, with every call traced.

The live sensory shield runs on the laptop and works offline. Video never leaves the device.
