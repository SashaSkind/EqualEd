# team-17

## Project

EqualEd spots sensory overload around an autistic student before it peaks, from their laptop camera: calming
sound in their AirPods, an instant alert on the teacher's phone, and a sensory map ranking overwhelming places
across the VAST video archive.

**Stack:** VAST VSS retrieval (explore, segments, search) over team-17's archive; NVIDIA Cosmos3-Reason captions +
YOLO11 counts used to score all 2,352 segments for sensory load; Cosmos Embed1 search; on the laptop: YOLO11-pose
+ ByteTrack, YOLOE open-vocabulary detection, YAMNet audio, Whisper; Python, OpenCV, Ultralytics.
